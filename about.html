HELLO, I’M MEGANATHAN

I connect business needs
with working software.

Based in Namakkal, Tamil Nadu, I develop, deploy, maintain, and support business web and mobile applications.

My experience spans ERP, CRM, HRM, trading, printing, and the paper industry. I work with Python, Django, Flutter, Dart, PostgreSQL, and MySQL, alongside Java, Spring Boot, React, and the web technologies that bring an interface to life. My broader focus includes payment gateway integration, third-party APIs and SDKs, map services, and AI-assisted solutions.

At Mahe Software Solutions, I gather and analyze client requirements, take responsibility for major development work, and coordinate a web development team of approximately five members. I also independently own the Flutter mobile application, from feature development and API integration to testing and updates.

I stay involved beyond implementation: resolving issues, deploying production updates, and supporting the software as business requirements evolve.
