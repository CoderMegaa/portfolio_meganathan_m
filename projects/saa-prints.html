01 / THE BIG PICTURE

Purpose & perspective.

SAA Prints supports the operational workflow of a sublimation printing business. The ERP covers customer job work, material handling, design selection, job cards, printing, fusing, delivery, and billing, reflecting the steps required to move a customer’s work through production.

THE OBJECTIVE

Represent the printing business’s customer and production workflow in software, from the incoming job through delivery and billing.

The business workflow

  1. 01Customer job & materials
  2. 02Design & job card
  3. 03Printing & fusing
  4. 04Delivery & billing
