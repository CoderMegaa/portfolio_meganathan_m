01 / THE BIG PICTURE

Purpose & perspective.

SAA TNPL is a trading-focused ERP application for bulk paper purchasing and customer sales. It supports purchase orders, sales orders, product management, and pricing workflows, including paper products with different sizes and GSM specifications.

THE OBJECTIVE

Build a complete ERP application around the purchasing and sales requirements of a bulk paper-trading business.

The business workflow

  1. 01Define paper specifications
  2. 02Manage bulk purchasing
  3. 03Manage pricing & sales orders
  4. 04Review trading records
