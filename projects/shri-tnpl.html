01 / THE BIG PICTURE

Purpose & perspective.

SHRI TNPL is a web application for managing the commission lifecycle associated with sales. It imports sales data, generates commissions based on imported sales values, tracks the associated customers, and supports commission allocation, distribution, and utilization. Time-bound schemes, related claims, reports, and ledgers form part of the same business workflow.

THE OBJECTIVE

Make the relationship between sales, customers, commission entitlements, distribution, schemes, and claims clear within one application.

The business workflow

  1. 01Import sales data
  2. 02Generate & link commissions
  3. 03Allocate, distribute & track use
  4. 04Manage schemes, claims & ledgers
02 / CORE FUNCTIONALITY

Built around real operations.

01

Sales imports & calculation

Import sales data and automatically generate commissions based on the imported sales value.

02

Allocation & utilization

Track the customers associated with commissions, allocate and distribute commissions to recipients, and record how commissions are utilized.

03

Time-bound schemes & claims

Create schemes with defined periods, manage them according to their durations, and handle commission claims associated with those schemes.

04

Reports & ledgers

Maintain reporting and ledger views covering commissions, schemes, and claims so the business can review the associated activity.

03 / UNDER THE HOOD

How it comes together.

Business workflow

Python and Django implement the sales, commission, scheme, and claim workflows described for the application.

Data & integration

PostgreSQL stores application data. REST APIs form part of the application’s technical stack.

Web interface

HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Bootstrap provide the browser-based interface for managing records and reviewing reports and ledgers.

MY CONTRIBUTION

Hands-on ownership.

Business value

Makes sales-linked commission activity traceable across calculation, allocation, utilization, schemes, and claims, with reports and ledgers supporting business review.
