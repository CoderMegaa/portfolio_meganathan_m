01 / THE BIG PICTURE

Purpose & perspective.

SAA Sublimation brings the operational needs of a sublimation-paper business into a connected ERP, CRM, and HRM environment. The business combines manufacturing with trading, so the application supports the journey from paper purchasing and production through product handling, sales, and billing, alongside customer and employee workflows.

THE OBJECTIVE

Bring interdependent manufacturing, trading, customer, and employee activities into a practical application that reflects how the business works.

The business workflow

  1. 01Purchase & product handling
  2. 02Production & trading
  3. 03Sales & billing
  4. 04Customer & employee follow-up

03 / UNDER THE HOOD

How it comes together.

Business logic

Python and Django implement the application workflows, with PostgreSQL storing operational records.

Web experience

HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Bootstrap provide the browser interface for business users.

Mobile connection

Python/Django REST APIs exchange JSON data with the associated SHRI Connect Flutter application. Firebase supports mobile push notifications.

MY CONTRIBUTION

Hands-on ownership.

I develop and maintain core ERP functionality, take ownership of major development requirements, and coordinate web requirements with the team. I independently manage the associated Flutter mobile application, including API integration and application updates.

Business value

Connects related business processes so purchasing, production, sales, customer management, and people operations can be supported within the same application environment.
